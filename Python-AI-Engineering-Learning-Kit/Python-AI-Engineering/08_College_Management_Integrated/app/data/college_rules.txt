COLLEGE MANAGEMENT POLICY – SAMPLE LEARNING DATA

ATTENDANCE
Students should maintain at least 75% attendance to be eligible for semester examinations. Condonation, medical leave, and other exceptions are governed by the official college regulations. This sample text is for software learning only.

FEES
Semester examination fees must be paid before the published examination deadline. Students should contact the accounts office for account-specific questions.

LIBRARY
Borrowed library books must be returned on or before the due date. Late returns may be subject to library rules.

EXAMINATIONS
Students should carry a valid college identity card during examinations and follow the official examination timetable.
